# Example2 
Input:  
Question: How does linguistic history contradict the idea that "news" is an acronym for "North, East, West, South"?

Output:  
News is not an acronym for compass directions. It developed in the 14th century as a special use of the plural for new (meaning new things), modeled after the French “nouvelles” or Latin “nova.”
